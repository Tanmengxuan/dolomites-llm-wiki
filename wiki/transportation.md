## Car Rental

- **Requirement**: Must bring **both** physical Singapore driving license and an International Driving Permit (IDP) — Italy requires the IDP alongside the SG licence, one per driver. If missing, AA Singapore issues one over the counter in ~1 hr (S$20, physical licence + NRIC) (source: 202609_dolomites.md, trip-checklist.md)
- **Provider**: Noleggiare, booking/doc no. **DG 114420** — Ford Focus automatic or similar, petrol/diesel, 5 seats, A/C
- **Rate**: €0 to pay at the desk, €0 damage excess, €0 theft excess, €100 deposit, unlimited km, additional driver included, Platinum Protection
- **Web check-in**: complete in advance at www.noleggiare.it/en/webcheckin-2/ (booking/doc no. + driver/licence details) — can halve counter time at the tight Mon 10:00 pickup (source: trip-checklist.md)
- Car is used throughout Part 1 (Sept 6–13) to drive between hiking areas
- On Sept 14, car is parked near the Cortina d'Ampezzo bus stop before taking the public bus to Lago di Braies
- **Pickup**: Mon 7 Sep 10:00 at Milan Linate. Desk is inside the Arrivals hall, ground floor, Rent-a-car Area; car is in Car Park "P2 Car Rental" (Viale Enrico Forlanini, Segrate). Running late? Call +39 020 997 8763 (desk open 07:30–23:00). Bring passport + SG licence + IDP (×2 drivers) + credit card + voucher (source: 202609_dolomites.md, trip-checklist.md)
- At handover: confirm full-to-full fuel policy **and** fuel type (gasolio vs benzina — [Italian pump colors don't indicate fuel type](https://www.facebook.com/heritanceitalyofficial/posts/filling-up-your-rental-car-in-italy-dont-trust-the-color-of-the-pump-handleand-t/1347462907407628/)), confirm both drivers on the contract, and video all panels/wheels/glass + odometer + fuel gauge before driving off (source: trip-checklist.md)
- **Return**: Milan airport, by 15:30 on Sept 19 at Noleggiare, Car Park P2 — refuel full near Linate first (Eni on Circonvallazione Idroscalo or Q8 on Via Mecenate, ~2 km out), photograph panels matching the pickup video, remove all luggage/kit, get a signed check-in report (source: trip-checklist.md)

### Driving Rules & Route Notes (source: trip-checklist.md)
- Route Sept 7: LIN → A51 → A4 → A22 Brenner → exit Chiusa-Val Gardena → Val di Funes (~325 km, ~4 hrs driving + breaks; Autogrill stops open 24/7)

## Entry Rules & Border Crossing (source: trip-checklist.md)

- **Visa**: visa-free (90/180 days)
- **ETIAS**: not required — not yet in operation. Beware scam "ETIAS" sites; the only official portal is travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en
- **EES biometrics**: live since 10 Apr 2026. Entry enrolment (photo + 4 fingerprints) happens at **Munich (MUC)** during the Mon 7 Sep transfer, not at Linate. Exit biometrics happen at **Frankfurt (FRA)** passport control on the Sat 19 Sep return transfer
- **VAT refund**: only worth pursuing for a single store receipt ≥ €154.94. Since LIN→FRA is inside Schengen, the EU exit point is FRA, not Linate — get the customs export stamp at the T1 Transit Area B customs office, then the Global Blue/Planet payout at T1 Area B L2 or Area C L2. Goods in hand luggage are the clean route; goods in checked baggage through-checked to SIN require export confirmation **before bag drop at Linate** instead
- See [[travel-documents]] for passport validity, IDP, and MFA registration details
