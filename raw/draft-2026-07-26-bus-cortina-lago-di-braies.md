# Bus Times: Cortina d'Ampezzo → Lago di Braies (Sept 14)

Researched 2026-07-26 via web search in response to trip-planning question about departure time for Alta Via 1 Day 1.

---

## Summary

Getting from Cortina d'Ampezzo to Lago di Braies on Sept 14 requires two buses with a connection in Dobbiaco. September 14 falls within the peak-season restricted window (July 1–September 15), meaning advance online booking is mandatory.

---

## Leg 1: Bus 445 — Cortina d'Ampezzo → Dobbiaco

- **Departs**: Cortina (Scuole/Olympic Stadium) at **08:20**
- **Arrives**: Dobbiaco Autostazione at approximately **09:05**
- **Journey time**: ~45 minutes
- **Ticket price**: ~€6.50 per person (2025 price; contactless and card accepted on board)
- Only the first two daily departures are practical for AV1 trekkers; later buses would arrive too late for a full hiking day.
- Tip: ask your Cortina hotel for a **South Tyrol Guest Card** — it gives free travel on the 445.
- Important: ride all the way to **Dobbiaco Autostazione** (the final stop) — do not exit early at Dobbiaco train station.

---

## Leg 2: Bus 442 — Dobbiaco → Lago di Braies

- **Frequency**: roughly every 30 minutes throughout the day
- **First departure**: 07:01 from Dobbiaco (arrives lake ~07:29)
- **After the 445 connection**: catch the 442 at approximately **09:10–09:30**, arriving Lago di Braies by **09:40–10:00**
- **Journey time**: ~30 minutes
- **Ticket price**: ~€7 per person (2025 price; 2026 price not yet released)

---

## Pre-Booking Requirement

Bus access to Lago di Braies is restricted between **July 1 and September 15**. During this window, advance online booking and prepayment are required. Tickets are typically released online around mid-June.

- **Pre-booking site**: https://www.prags.bz/en
- September 14 falls inside this window — do not attempt to board without a pre-booked ticket.

---

## Recommended Departure Plan

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 08:00 | Head to Cortina bus stop (Scuole/Stadio) |
| 08:20 | Board Bus 445 toward Dobbiaco |
| ~09:05 | Arrive Dobbiaco Autostazione |
| ~09:10–09:30 | Board Bus 442 toward Lago di Braies |
| ~09:40–10:00 | Arrive Lago di Braies — start hiking! |

This schedule provides ample time to reach Rifugio Fodara Vedla before its 14:00 check-in.

---

## Action Items

1. Book Bus 442 at https://www.prags.bz/en from mid-June onwards — tickets sell out.
2. Confirm the 445 timetable at https://www.suedtirolmobil.info closer to the trip date.
3. Ask the Cortina hotel for a South Tyrol Guest Card for free travel on the 445.
