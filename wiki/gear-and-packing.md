## Via Ferrata Equipment (Sept 10)

[[via-ferrata]] activities require a via ferrata set (harness + lanyard) and helmet. The plan is to **rent** this gear rather than buy it.

**Resolved**: **Intersport Val Gardena** — full kit €27/day, pickup at Selva (Nives/Ciampinoi) or Santa Cristina (Dosses). Alternatives: Sport Bruno Riffeser (Selva), or Corvara/Colfosco shops, €20–30/day (source: trip-checklist.md).

## Cash

Bring EUR cash to pay for hut stays during the [[alta-via-1]] section (source: 202609_dolomites.md). Cash target €800–900 for two — see [[budget]] money plan.

## Full Packing List (source: trip-checklist.md)

### Clothing (per person; no cotton on trail)
- 2–3 merino/synthetic tees + 1 long-sleeve; fleece/alpha mid-layer; packable down jacket
- Hardshell jacket **and** pants
- Thermal base layer (top + bottom) for summits/hut nights; hiking pants ×2 + shorts/tights
- Beanie + light gloves (for Piz Boè/Marmolada), buff ×2, sun hat
- 4× underwear, 3× hiking socks + spares; camp socks
- Broken-in waterproof boots (non-negotiable) + camp shoes/flip-flops for hut showers/dorms
- Bologna evening outfit (1 shirt) + underwear for the 19th — left in the car until Monday
- Sleepwear for shared dorms

### Hiking & safety
- Trekking poles (collapsible — checked bag only)
- Via ferrata kit (see above)
- Daypack 25–35 L + hut pack 35–45 L + rain covers + dry bags/stuff sacks
- 2 L water capacity each + filter or purification tablets (fountains/hut taps unreliable in Sept)
- Cat 3–4 sunglasses, SPF50+, SPF lip balm
- First aid: Compeed + tape, ibuprofen/paracetamol, antihistamine, loperamide, ORS, elastic bandage, wound strips, **tick tweezers**, space blanket, whistle; personal meds (full trip supply + doctor's letter, in cabin); motion-sickness tabs if lifts bother you
- Headlamp (red mode), earplugs + eye mask (shared dorms), silk **sleeping-bag liner (mandatory at huts)**, quick-dry towel
- Snacks + electrolyte tabs, ziplock bags, duct tape wrapped on a card, spare lace, TP, small trash bag
- 20,000 mAh power bank each (**carry-on only**, ≤100 Wh, max 2 per person, never used/charged in flight), multi-port USB charger
- Paper Tabacco maps (sheet 03 Cortina; sheet 05 Val Gardena) — GPS backup
- ❌ **Drone stays home** — banned in Puez-Odle, Fanes-Senes-Braies, Ampezzo parks, and at Braies

### Electronics & connectivity
- Adapters: Italy uses Type F/L, 230 V — bring 3 (2 people charging at huts); car USB-C dual charger + cables
- eSIMs installed before departure (home Wi-Fi), activated on landing — see [[travel-documents]]
- Offline downloads: Google Maps (all area regions), AllTrails offline (all saved hikes), GPX of the 5-day AV1 into Organic Maps/OsmAnd/Outdooractive, SuedtirolMobil app, weather apps (Windy, Bergfex, 3Bmeteo, meteomountains.eu), saved webcams (Seceda livecam)

## Activity-Specific Requirements

### Hut-to-Hut ([[alta-via-1]], Sept 14–17)
Multi-night hut stay with half-board meals at some huts. Pack light — one 35–45 L pack each, ≤6–8 kg (source: trip-checklist.md). Shared facilities at some rifugios. Cash-based payments — see above.
