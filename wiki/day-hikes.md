# Day Hikes

**Summary**: All day hikes planned for Part 1 of the trip (Sept 6–13), covering five distinct areas of the Dolomites.

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

## Val di Funes (Sept 7–8)

### Viewpoint Santa Maddalena (Sept 7 — warm-up)
- Duration: 1.5 hrs round trip
- Easy intro hike; Sept 6 is now a pure arrival/travel day with no hike (source: 202609_dolomites.md)
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/punto-panoramico-santa-maddalena

### Adolf Munkel Trail (Sept 8 — option A)
- Duration: 3.5 hrs loop
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/via-delle-odle-zanser-alm-rifugio-delle-odle-malga-dusler

### Tullen Summit (Sept 8 — option B)
- Duration: 6 hrs
- More demanding; full summit day
- Guide: https://www.moonhoneytravel.com/tullen-peak-hike-odle-d-eores-dolomites/

Choose one of Adolf Munkel or Tullen for Sept 8.

## Seceda (Sept 9)

### Santa Cristina → Rifugio Firenze → Seceda
- Duration: 4 hrs
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/santa-cristina-rifugio-firenze-seceda
- Seceda is one of the iconic Dolomite ridgelines
- Check the [Seceda livecam](https://www.valgardena.it/en/webcams/seceda-live/) before going (source: 202609_dolomites.md)
- Drive: [Hotel Kabis → Santa Cristina](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Hotel+Kabis,+Peterweg,+14,+39040+San+Pietro+BZ,+Italy/Parkplatz,+Ruacia+Str.,+39048+S%C3%ABlva,+Autonome+Provinz+Bozen+-+S%C3%BCdtirol,+Italy/@46.6066847,11.5463788,12z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x477811b8554913cf:0x436feaee9d87edae!2m2!1d11.6800749!2d46.6423316!1m5!1m1!1s0x477814994b68e629:0xe45cdce15252f3b1!2m2!1d11.7288925!2d46.556967!3e0), ~1 hr
- Descent via Chairlift Fermeda then Col Raiser Gondola back to Santa Cristina; passes can be bought on the day (source: 202609_dolomites.md)

## Sassolungo (Sept 11)

### Giro del Gruppo del Sassolungo
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/giro-del-gruppo-del-sassolungo
- Circuit around the Sassolungo massif
- Chosen over Tre Cime circuit: less crowded, more huts along the route, comparable views (source: 202609_dolomites.md)
- Drive: [Garni B&B Mozart Nesthouse → trailhead](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Mozart+NestHouse,+Via+Roma,+27,+38032+Canazei+TN,+Italy/Parcheggio,+39048+Selva+di+Val+Gardena+BZ,+Italy/@46.4908723,11.7536313,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x47786a69bb6f5c5d:0x1c4ff5f5c4c83200!2m2!1d11.7659692!2d46.476781!1m5!1m1!1s0x47786bfc29fd13a7:0xd1dd03c51b3eb4e4!2m2!1d11.7576674!2d46.5093389!3e0), ~30 mins

## Piz Boe (Sept 12)

### Piz Boe Summit
- Guide: https://inafarawayland.com/piz-boe-summit-hike/
- Drive: [Garni B&B Mozart Nesthouse → Passo Pordoi](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Mozart+NestHouse,+Via+Roma,+27,+38032+Canazei+TN,+Italy/Parking+Passo+Pordoi,+38032+Canazei+TN,+Italy/@46.4841513,11.7778886,15z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x47786a69bb6f5c5d:0x1c4ff5f5c4c83200!2m2!1d11.7659692!2d46.476781!1m5!1m1!1s0x477841e45c1e738b:0xeb51b5d8851f7ca7!2m2!1d11.8104543!2d46.488107!3e0), ~30 mins, then cable car to Rifugio Maria
- AllTrails map from Rifugio Maria: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/trentino/piz-boe-via-sasso-pordoi
- Optional harder variant: Ferrata Piazzetta (difficult via ferrata)
  - AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/veneto/ferrata-piazzetta-piz-boe
- See [[via-ferrata]] for ferrata context
