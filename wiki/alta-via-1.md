# Alta Via 1

**Summary**: The hut-to-hut backbone of the trip — a 4-night partial traverse of Alta Via 1, starting at Lago di Braies and ending at Rifugio Croda da Lago (Sept 14–17).

## Overview

Alta Via 1 is one of the classic long-distance hiking routes through the Dolomites. This trip covers a partial section over 4 nights (Sept 14–17), moving south from Lago di Braies toward Cortina d'Ampezzo.

## Getting to the Start

On Sept 14, the car is parked near the Cortina d'Ampezzo bus stop (two free parking options identified). A public bus runs to Lago di Braies via Südtirol Mobilität.
