## Via Ferrata Brigata Tridentina al Pisciadu (Sept 10)

- **When**: Sept 10 (Thu)
- **AllTrails**: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/via-ferrata-brigata-tridentina-al-pisciadu
- **AllTrails custom map**: https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/via-ferrata-brigata-tridentina-37cfaf0
- **Guide**: https://inafarawayland.com/via-ferrata-brigata-tridentina/
- Drive: [Hotel Garni Morene → trailhead](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Garni+Morene,+Streda+Puez,+49,+39048+Selva+di+Val+Gardena+BZ,+Italy/Parking+M%C3%ABisules,+Passo+Gardena,+39033+S%C3%ABlva,+Autonome+Provinz+Bozen+-+S%C3%BCdtirol,+Italy/@46.5455194,11.7594592,14z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x477814e5364f049d:0xb57aae3f3457296e!2m2!1d11.7610673!2d46.5587348!1m5!1m1!1s0x47781520be5eabcf:0xa54d0e218aa93ff7!2m2!1d11.8092182!2d46.5499317!3e0) (Passo Gardena parking), ~30 mins (source: 202609_dolomites.md)

### Highlights
- Famous for its **suspension bridge**
- Rifugio at the summit for a meal break
- Good summit views

### Planning note
The source notes this should be done before [[sassolungo]] (Sassolungo is Sept 11) because the Brigata Tridentina is geographically closer to the [[seceda]] area (source: 202609_dolomites.md). This ordering is already reflected in the [[itinerary]].

## Gear Requirements

Via ferrata requires specific safety gear beyond normal hiking equipment. Needed (see [[gear-and-packing]]):
- Via ferrata set (harness + lanyard)
- Helmet
- Gloves (recommended)

The plan is to **rent** this equipment rather than buy it — finding a rental/harness shop is an open todo (source: 202609_dolomites.md). See [[open-todos]].
