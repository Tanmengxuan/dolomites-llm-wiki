## Overview

Alta Via 1 is one of the classic long-distance hiking routes through the Dolomites. This trip covers a partial section over 4 nights (Sept 14–17), moving south from Lago di Braies toward Cortina d'Ampezzo.

## Getting to the Start

On Sept 14, the car is parked near the Cortina d'Ampezzo bus stop (two free parking options identified — see [[transportation]]). The journey to Lago di Braies requires two buses:

1. **Bus 445** departs Cortina (Scuole/Olympic Stadium) at **08:20**, arriving Dobbiaco Autostazione at ~09:05 (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/)
2. **Bus 442** departs Dobbiaco at ~09:10–09:30, arriving Lago di Braies at **~09:40–10:00**

**Recommended departure**: leave for the bus stop by **08:00**.

This gives a full hiking day with comfortable buffer before [[rifugio-fodara-vedla]] check-in at 14:00.

> ⚠️ **Pre-booking required**: Sept 14 falls in the peak restricted window (July 1–Sept 15). Bus 442 tickets must be pre-booked at https://www.prags.bz/en from mid-June — they sell out. See [[draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies]] for full details.

After the traverse ends at Rifugio Croda da Lago on Sept 17, the route back to the car has not been detailed in the source — needs confirmation.
