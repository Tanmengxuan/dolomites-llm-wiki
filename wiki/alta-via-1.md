## Overview

Alta Via 1 is one of the classic long-distance hiking routes through the Dolomites. This trip covers a partial section over 4 nights (Sept 14–17), moving south from Lago di Braies toward Cortina d'Ampezzo. The route follows a "[RandyOrTan] 5-day Alta Via 1 Itinerary" — [AllTrails custom route](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/copy-5-day-alta-via-1-itinerary-f0e52b8) (source: 202609_dolomites.md).

## Getting to the Start

On Sept 14, the car is parked near the Cortina d'Ampezzo bus stop (see [[transportation]] for updated parking options — the previous free long-stay lot is closed for 2026 Olympics works). First ask Hotel Menardi if they'll keep the car; otherwise use Parcheggio Multipiano Pontechiesa or the free Lungo Boite lot (source: trip-checklist.md). The journey to Lago di Braies requires two buses (also referred to in the source as "bus from Toblach to Lago di Braies", Toblach being the German name for Dobbiaco):

1. **Bus 445** departs Cortina (Stadio Olimpico del Ghiaccio / "Scuole" stop) at **08:05**, arriving Toblach at **08:50**
2. **Bus 442** departs Toblach at **09:01**, arriving Lago di Braies at **09:29** — must be pre-booked at https://www.prags.bz/ticket

> ⚠️ **Contradiction noted**: an earlier draft source (`draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md`) gave 08:20/~09:40–10:00 for this connection. `trip-checklist.md` states its times are "verified in the official 2026 timetables" and is treated as authoritative here — see [[transportation]] for the full comparison.

> ⚠️ **Pre-booking required**: Sept 14 falls in the peak restricted window (July 1–Sept 15). Bus 442 tickets must be pre-booked with prepayment at https://www.prags.bz/ticket — they sell out. See [[draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies]] for background.

### Repacking to hut mode (source: trip-checklist.md)

Sunday (Sept 13) night: repack into one 35–45 L pack each, **≤6–8 kg** (huts provide blankets; max 10 kg). Lock big duffels and Bologna-bound clothes in the car; keep hut kit + documents + cash + meds + insurance on your back.

### Day 1 lunch/weather contingency plan (source: 202609_dolomites.md)

- Check weather in advance. If good: lunch at Lago di Braies, then hike directly to [Rifugio Fodara Vedla](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7fbK6Nyso8B5t9pAA). If bad in the afternoon: hike faster to Sennes/Fodara Vedla for lunch instead.
- The 9:00 or 9:30am bus reaches the trailhead ~10:00–10:30am. At a fast pace, Rifugio Biella is reached for lunch ~13:30–14:00; at a slower pace, ~14:00–15:00+.
- Alternative: eat lunch at Lago di Braies at ~11:00–11:30am, then hike 5+ hrs directly to Rifugio Fodara Vedla (more rushed).

After the traverse ends at Rifugio Croda da Lago on Sept 17, the group drives [Cortina → Bologna](https://maps.app.goo.gl/px9ywHuK8n5eg1gd9) on Sept 18 (~4 hrs) — see [[itinerary]].

Key notes:
- **Rifugio Fodara Vedla**: Private room, half board, EUR 196 remaining payment
- **Scotoni Hutte**: Shared room, half board, EUR 72 remaining payment
- **Rifugio 5 Torri**: Private room (shared toilet/shower), half board, EUR 144 remaining payment
- **Rifugio Croda da Lago**: Shared room, half board, EUR 96 remaining payment

Bring EUR cash for hut payments — huts along the route are cash-based (source: 202609_dolomites.md). Croda da Lago takes cards/POS, but assume cash works everywhere and cards sometimes don't (source: trip-checklist.md).

## Daily Trek Notes (source: trip-checklist.md)

- **Morning**: poles on, 2 L water, packed lunch collected (order the night before, ~€8–14), check weather (Windy offline tiles or ask hut staff)
- **Evenings**: confirm the next hut by WhatsApp, order breakfast time + packed lunch, charge devices early (sockets scarce), dry socks/shoes in the boot room, quiet hours 22:00–06:00
- **Hut etiquette**: boots off at the door (clogs provided), a **sleeping-bag liner is mandatory** (bring your own silk/cotton; some huts rent €5–10), towels are not provided (bring a quick-dry towel), showers are coin/token €2–5 where they exist (Fodara Vedla's private rooms have their own shower/WC), no trekking boots in the dining room
- **Water**: don't assume hut tap water is drinkable (5 Torri's is; many huts sell bottled) — carry a filter or purification tablets and buy bottles at huts. Drinks at dinner are **not** included in half board (beer €5–7, water €4–5)
- **Waymarking**: the AV1 is followed via numbered trails (1 → 20B → …); AV1 branding is sparse early on. Use GPX + a paper map, don't rely on signage alone — **Tabacco 1:25k sheet 03 "Cortina d'Ampezzo"** covers this whole trek (buy at Cortina tourist info/mountain shops, ~€8–10)
- **Thunderstorm protocol**: get off ridges early; if caught out, get off any ferrata cable immediately, descend below treeline, spread out 10+ m, poles down, wait 30 min after the last thunder. Emergency: **112** (say "soccorso alpino", give coordinates from phone/watch) — hut staff can also call and shelter you
- Fri 18 Sep: breakfast at Croda da Lago 07:00–08:30, out by ~09:00 for the Passo Giau/Cortina leg, pick up the car, aim for Bologna by ~17:00–18:00
