# Trip Overview

**Summary**: A 14-day trip to the Dolomites, Italy for 2 people in September 2026, combining day hikes from hotel bases and a multi-day hut-to-hut traverse.

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-02

---

## Basic Facts

- **Destination**: Dolomites, Italy (South Tyrol region)
- **Dates**: September 6–19, 2026
- **Duration**: 14 days
- **Group size**: 2 people
- **Home base**: Singapore (SG driving license + International driving license required for car rental)

## Trip Structure

The trip is split into two distinct phases:

**Part 1 — Day Hikes (Sept 6–13)**
Based out of hotels, driving between areas. Covers [[val-di-funes]], [[seceda]], [[sassolungo]], [[piz-boe]], and a [[via-ferrata]] day. Sept 9 is a planned rest day.

**Part 2 — Hut-to-Hut (Sept 14–17)**
A 4-night partial [[alta-via-1]] traverse starting from Lago di Braies and ending at Rifugio Croda da Lago. Car is parked near the Cortina bus stop; public bus taken to the trailhead.

**Wind-down (Sept 18–19)**
Day trip to Lake Garda on Sept 18, then departure on Sept 19 from a Bologna Airbnb.
