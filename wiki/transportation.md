## Public Bus — Lago di Braies (Sept 14)

- **Route**: Cortina d'Ampezzo → Lago di Braies (two-leg journey via Dobbiaco)
- **Service**: Südtirol Mobilität
- **Website**: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/
- Full schedule details: [[draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies]]

### Leg 1 — Bus 445: Cortina → Dobbiaco

- Departs Cortina (Scuole/Olympic Stadium): **08:20** (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/)
- Arrives Dobbiaco Autostazione: ~09:05
- Journey time: ~45 minutes; ticket ~€6.50 pp (2025 price)
- Stay on to **Dobbiaco Autostazione** — do not exit at Dobbiaco train station early
- South Tyrol Guest Card (free from Cortina hotel) covers Bus 445 travel

### Leg 2 — Bus 442: Dobbiaco → Lago di Braies

- After connection, board at ~09:10–09:30; arrives Lago di Braies **~09:40–10:00**
- Runs every ~30 minutes; journey ~30 minutes; ticket ~€7 pp (2025 price)

### Pre-booking (MANDATORY for Sept 14)

Sept 14 falls in the restricted window (July 1–September 15). Tickets must be booked and prepaid online from ~mid-June — they sell out (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.prags.bz/en).

- **Book Bus 442 at**: https://www.prags.bz/en
- Confirm Bus 445 timetable at: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/

### Recommended departure plan

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 08:00 | Head to Cortina bus stop (Scuole/Stadio) |
| 08:20 | Board Bus 445 toward Dobbiaco |
| ~09:05 | Arrive Dobbiaco Autostazione |
| ~09:10–09:30 | Board Bus 442 toward Lago di Braies |
| ~09:40–10:00 | Arrive Lago di Braies — start hiking! |
