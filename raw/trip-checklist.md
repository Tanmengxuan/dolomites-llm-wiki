## **2\. Week 1 — day-hike base prep (Mon 7 – Sun 13 Sep)**

* ☐ **Daily rhythm** (the weather rule that makes or breaks Dolomites Sept): trailhead by 07:30–08:30 · high point by 11:00–12:00 · **off summits/ridges/ferratas by \~14:00** (convective storms 14:00–16:00). Check **Windy/Bergfex/3Bmeteo \+ webcam** every evening; you already kept 9–12 Sep flexible — use it.

* ☐ **Mon 7 Sep \= arrival day** (land 09:10 LIN → \~4 h drive → Kabis): no hike planned. If skies are clear and legs feel OK, the Santa Maddalena viewpoint warm-up (1.5 h) still works late afternoon — otherwise it slides to Tue. **Grocery run Mon evening or Tue morning**: 6×1.5 L water, trail snacks, fruit/nuts, sandwich supplies, SPF refill.

* ☐ **Tue 8 Sep (Adolf Munkel / Tullen)** and the Odle days: fountains on trail are unreliable by September — carry **2 L each**; refill in villages (mapped refill points in Val Gardena).

* ☐ **Wed 9 Sep (Seceda)**: you ascend on foot from Santa Cristina; **descent lifts: Fermeda chair \+ Col Raiser gondola**. 2026 note: Seceda lifts use **online time-slot pre-booking** for the Ortisei side — for your descent just buy at the station ("Seceda Premium" combo incl. Fermeda from \~€45/p return-equivalent); Dolomiti Supersummer passes are **not valid** on these lifts; **last rides \~17:00–17:30** — be at Fermeda by 16:30.

* ☐ **Thu 10 Sep (via ferrata Brigata Tridentina)**: grade C, \~400 m of cable, famous suspension bridge, \~4.5–6 h loop from the parking below **Passo Gardena**. Officially required: **harness \+ energy-absorbing via ferrata set \+ helmet** — **rent tonight/Monday: Intersport Val Gardena full kit €27/day** (pickup Selva — Nives/Ciampinoi — or S. Cristina — Dosses; also Sport Bruno Riffeser in Selva; Corvara/Colfosco shops €20–30/day). Start by 08:00; the only bail-out is before the bridge — after it you finish. Zero storm tolerance: steel cable \+ lightning \= never; if thunder threatens early, swap to Gran Cir (2.5 h, easy) or a valley walk. First-timers can add a guide (dolomagic.it, catores.com, altabadiaguides.com).

* ☐ **Fri 11 Sep (Sassolungo circuit)**: long day (\~17 km) — earliest start of the week; the Forcella Sassolungo "coffin" gondola is a mid-circuit bail-out option.

* ☐ **Sat 12 Sep (Piz Boè)**: Pordoi cable car runs (season to 1 Nov; 09:00–17:00; return €32/p). Summit 3,152 m \= **0–8 °C with wind**: puffer \+ gloves \+ beanie in the pack. Back at the top station by \~16:30 for the last car down.

* ☐ **Sun 13 Sep**: Marmolada (cable car verified running to **27 Sep 2026**; first ascent 09:00, **last descent 16:30**; return €38–40 pp; terrace needs no special gear, just warm layers \+ cat-3/4 sunglasses) **or** Cortina rest day — decide on the Saturday-evening forecast. Then drive to Cortina, check into **Hotel Menardi**.

* ☐ **Rolling admin all week**: WhatsApp-confirm each hut 1–2 days ahead (ETA 16:00–17:00 \+ any dietary requests — say vegetarian/other at check-in too); keep cash ≥€600 through the week; back up photos to cloud on hotel Wi-Fi; charge power banks nightly.

## **4\. On the trek (14–18 Sep) — daily micro-checklist**

* ☐ Morning: poles on, 2 L water, packed lunch collected (order **the night before**, \~€8–14), weather glance (Windy offline tiles / ask hut staff — they know).

* ☐ Evenings: confirm next hut (WhatsApp), order breakfast time \+ packed lunch, charge devices early (sockets scarce), dry socks/shoes in the boot room, **quiet hours 22:00–06:00**.

* ☐ Hut etiquette: boots off at the door (clogs provided), **sleeping-bag liner is mandatory** (bring your own silk/cotton; some huts rent €5–10), **towels not provided** (quick-dry towel), showers coin/token €2–5 where they exist (Fodara private rooms have own shower/WC — luxury, enjoy), no trekking in boots in the dining room.

* ☐ Water: **don't assume hut tap water is drinkable** (5 Torri's is; many huts sell bottled). Filter/tablets \+ buy bottles at huts. Drinks at dinner are **not** in half board (beer €5–7, water €4–5 — it's all heli-lifted, prices are normal).

* ☐ Waymarks: the AV1 is followed via numbered trails (1 → 20B → …), AV1 branding is sparse early on — **GPX \+ paper map, don't wing it**: **Tabacco 1:25k sheet 03 "Cortina d'Ampezzo"** covers your entire trek (buy at Cortina tourist info/mountain shops \~€8–10; sheet 05 Val Gardena for week 1).

* ☐ Thunderstorm protocol: off ridges early; if caught — off the ferrata cable immediately, descend below treeline, spread out 10+ m, poles down, wait 30 min after last thunder. **Emergency: 112** (say "soccorso alpino", give coordinates from your phone/watch). Hut staff can call and shelter you.

* ☐ Fri 18 Sep: breakfast (Croda da Lago 07:00–08:30), out by \~09:00 → **Passo Giau/Cortina** leg per your plan → pick up car → **Bologna by \~17:00–18:00**.

## **8\. Health & safety summary**

* **Insurance \+ Aiut Alpin** (§0) \= your rescue cover. Carry policy numbers \+ 24 h hotlines offline.

* **Altitude**: max sleeping \~2,100 m (low AMS risk); day max 3,265 m lift-assisted — hydrate, easy on days 1–2, descend if headache+nausea worsens.

* **Ticks (TBE exists in Südtirol/Trentino)**: trousers tucked, repellent, daily tick check (groin/hairline/armpits), tweezers in kit; no raw/unpasteurised milk-cheese from farms.

* **Sun at altitude**: SPF50 every morning at the hut; sunglasses on even when cloudy.

* **Storms**: the 14:00 rule (§2). On ferrata in any thunder risk: don't start / turn back.

* **Water**: valley tap \= safe; mountain fountains seasonal; hut taps only where signed potabile; treat/filter otherwise.

* Medicines: original packaging \+ doctor's letter; controlled substances — check Italian import rules if applicable.
