## Money Plan (source: trip-checklist.md)

Cash target for the couple: **€800–900**, changed before/on arrival (Changi late-night changers for a first tranche, ATM top-ups via YouTrip/Wise/Revolut — always choose EUR at the terminal, never SGD/DCC). Ask for small notes (10s/20s/50s); the only ATM near the trek corridor is at Passo Falzarego.
