# Sassolungo

**Summary**: A prominent massif in the Val Gardena area; the circuit hike around it is planned for Sept 10 as an alternative to the more crowded Tre Cime route.

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-02

---

Sassolungo (Langkofel in German) is a striking group of peaks at the head of Val Gardena and Alpe di Siusi.

## Planned Hike (Sept 10)

- **Route**: Giro del Gruppo del Sassolungo (circuit)
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/giro-del-gruppo-del-sassolungo

## Why Sassolungo over Tre Cime?

The source notes that the Sassolungo circuit was chosen over the Tre Cime circuit because it is less crowded, has more huts along the route, and maintains comparable views (source: 202609_dolomites.md).
