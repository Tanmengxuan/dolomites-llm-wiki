# Val di Funes

**Summary**: A scenic valley in South Tyrol, used as the base for the first two days of hiking (Sept 6–7).

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-05-02

---

Val di Funes (Villnöss in German) sits beneath the Odle/Geisler group. It is known for the church of Santa Maddalena (needs verification — not from source).

## Planned Hikes

### Sept 6 — Viewpoint Santa Maddalena (warm-up)
- 1.5 hrs round trip
- Easy first-day hike to get acclimatised

### Sept 7 — Choose one:
- **Adolf Munkel Trail** — 3.5 hrs loop through the Odle foothills
- **Tullen Summit** — 6 hrs; full summit day with more elevation
