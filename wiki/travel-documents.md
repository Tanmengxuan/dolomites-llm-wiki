## Passports & Visas

- Visa-free entry (90/180 days)
- Passport validity must be ≥3 months beyond the 20 Sep 2026 return **and** issued <10 years ago (Schengen rule)
- Take a photo of each passport → cloud/email; print return booking, all hotels, hut confirmations, Airbnb, rental voucher, and insurance

## International Driving Permit (IDP)

Italy requires an IDP alongside the Singapore driving licence, and the Noleggiare desk at Linate will ask for it — one per driver (the included additional driver must also carry a licence + IDP).

- **If you have it**: pack it in hand luggage with the licence
- **If you don't**: AA Singapore issues one over the counter in ~1 hr (S$20, physical licence + NRIC)
- Without an IDP the group cannot legally drive this trip — the fallback is trains (LIN→Milan→Bolzano, then buses/Südtirol mobil) or private transfers, and cancelling the rental (doc DG 114420) before the Mon 10:00 pickup to limit fees

See [[transportation]] for the rest of the car rental details.

## Entry Rules (EU border)

- **ETIAS**: not required — not yet in operation. The only official portal is travel-europe.europa.eu/etias_en; beware scam "ETIAS" sites
- **EES biometrics**: live since 10 Apr 2026. Entry enrolment (photo + 4 fingerprints) happens at **Munich (MUC)** during the Mon 7 Sep transfer, not at Linate. Exit biometrics happen at **Frankfurt (FRA)** passport control on the Sat 19 Sep return transfer
- **VAT refund**: only worth it for a single receipt ≥€154.94; the exit point is FRA, not Linate — see [[transportation]] for the customs process

## Registration & Emergency Prep

- **MFA eRegister** (eregister.mfa.gov.sg) — register before departure, 2 min
- Duty office 24/7: +65 6379 8800 / 8855
- Honorary Consulate-General of Singapore, Rome (Via Frattina 89) — via rome.mfa.gov.sg
- See [[insurance-and-safety]] for insurance, rescue cover, and the full emergency contacts card

## Car Rental Admin

- **Noleggiare web check-in**: complete at www.noleggiare.it/en/webcheckin-2/ with booking/doc no. **DG 114420** + driver/licence details before arrival — halves counter time at the tight Mon 10:00 pickup
- See [[transportation]] for the full car rental writeup

## Connectivity

- **eSIMs**: install on home Wi-Fi before departure, activate on landing. Options considered: Nomad Italy 10 GB/30 days (~S$23.54, Iliad/WINDTre) or Holafly Italy 15 days (~US$23.90, Vodafone/WINDTre — warns mountain coverage can dip)
- **SG SIM**: keep roaming ON for voice/SMS only (needed for OTPs), data roaming OFF on the SG line
- **Bank apps**: enable travel notices, push notifications ON, note 24 hr card hotlines offline
- **Offline downloads**: Google Maps for all area regions, AllTrails offline (needs AllTrails+), GPX of the 5-day AV1 into Organic Maps/OsmAnd/Outdooractive, SuedtirolMobil app, weather apps (Windy, Bergfex, 3Bmeteo, meteomountains.eu), saved webcams (Seceda livecam) — see [[gear-and-packing]]
