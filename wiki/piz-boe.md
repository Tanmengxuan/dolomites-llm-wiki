# Piz Boe

**Summary**: A summit hike in the Sella Group planned for Sept 12, with an optional difficult via ferrata variant.

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-01

---

Piz Boe is the highest peak in the Sella Group and one of the more accessible high summits in the central Dolomites.

## Planned Hike (Sept 12)

- **Route**: Piz Boe summit
- Guide: https://inafarawayland.com/piz-boe-summit-hike/
- Drive: [Garni B&B Mozart Nesthouse → Passo Pordoi](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Mozart+NestHouse,+Via+Roma,+27,+38032+Canazei+TN,+Italy/Parking+Passo+Pordoi,+38032+Canazei+TN,+Italy/@46.4841513,11.7778886,15z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x47786a69bb6f5c5d:0x1c4ff5f5c4c83200!2m2!1d11.7659692!2d46.476781!1m5!1m1!1s0x477841e45c1e738b:0xeb51b5d8851f7ca7!2m2!1d11.8104543!2d46.488107!3e0), ~30 mins, then cable car to Rifugio Maria (source: 202609_dolomites.md)
- AllTrails map from Rifugio Maria: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/trentino/piz-boe-via-sasso-pordoi

### Optional Variant — Ferrata Piazzetta
- **Difficulty**: Difficult (via ferrata)
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/veneto/ferrata-piazzetta-piz-boe
