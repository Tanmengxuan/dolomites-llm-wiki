# Seceda

**Summary**: An iconic ridgeline in the Val Gardena area, planned for Sept 9 via a hike from Santa Cristina through Rifugio Firenze.

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md, raw/trip-checklist.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-06

---

Seceda is one of the most recognisable viewpoints in the Dolomites — a long, craggy ridge above Val Gardena. It can be reached by gondola or on foot.

## Planned Hike (Sept 9)

- **Route**: Santa Cristina → Rifugio Firenze → Seceda
- Duration: 4 hrs
- AllTrails: https://www.alltrails.com/trail/italy/south-tyrol/santa-cristina-rifugio-firenze-seceda
- Check the [Seceda livecam](https://www.valgardena.it/en/webcams/seceda-live/) before going (source: 202609_dolomites.md)
- Drive: [Hotel Kabis → Santa Cristina](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Hotel+Kabis,+Peterweg,+14,+39040+San+Pietro+BZ,+Italy/Parkplatz,+Ruacia+Str.,+39048+S%C3%ABlva,+Autonome+Provinz+Bozen+-+S%C3%BCdtirol,+Italy/@46.6066847,11.5463788,12z/data=!3m1!4b1!4m14!4m13!1m5!1m1!1s0x477811b8554913cf:0x436feaee9d87edae!2m2!1d11.6800749!2d46.6423316!1m5!1m1!1s0x477814994b68e629:0xe45cdce15252f3b1!2m2!1d11.7288925!2d46.556967!3e0), ~1 hr

### Descent
- Chairlift Fermeda, then Col Raiser Gondola, back to Santa Cristina
- Lift passes can be bought on the day itself (source: 202609_dolomites.md) — see [[open-todos]]
- **2026 update**: the Ortisei-side lifts use online time-slot pre-booking; buy the "Seceda Premium" combo (incl. Fermeda) at the station, ~€45 pp return-equivalent. Dolomiti Supersummer passes are **not valid** on these lifts. Last rides ~17:00–17:30 — be at Fermeda by 16:30 (source: trip-checklist.md)

## Planning note

The source notes that the [[via-ferrata]] Brigata Tridentina (Sept 10) should be done before [[sassolungo]] (Sept 11) as it is geographically closer to the Seceda area — this ordering is already reflected in the [[itinerary]].
