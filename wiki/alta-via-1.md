## Overview

Alta Via 1 is one of the classic long-distance hiking routes through the Dolomites. This trip covers a partial section over 4 nights (Sept 14–17), moving south from Lago di Braies toward Cortina d'Ampezzo. The route follows a "[RandyOrTan] 5-day Alta Via 1 Itinerary" — [AllTrails custom route](https://www.alltrails.com/explore/custom-routes/copy-5-day-alta-via-1-itinerary-f0e52b8) (source: 202609_dolomites.md).

## Getting to the Start

On Sept 14, the car is parked near the Cortina d'Ampezzo bus stop. Free parking options identified (source: 202609_dolomites.md) — see [[transportation]] — or ask the hotel about parking with them for the few nights. The journey to Lago di Braies requires two buses (also referred to in the source as "bus from Toblach to Lago di Braies", Toblach being the German name for Dobbiaco):

1. **Bus 445** departs Cortina (Scuole/Olympic Stadium) at **08:20**, arriving Dobbiaco Autostazione at ~09:05 (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/)
2. **Bus 442** departs Dobbiaco at ~09:10–09:30, arriving Lago di Braies at **~09:40–10:00** — must be pre-booked at https://www.prags.bz/en/offer/detail/shuttle-pragser-wildsee-lake_21

**Recommended departure**: leave for the bus stop by **08:00**, aiming for the 9:00 or 9:30am bus.

> ⚠️ **Pre-booking required**: Sept 14 falls in the peak restricted window (July 1–Sept 15). Bus 442 tickets must be pre-booked at https://www.prags.bz/en from mid-June — they sell out. See [[draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies]] for full details.

### Day 1 lunch/weather contingency plan (source: 202609_dolomites.md)

- Check weather in advance. If good: lunch at Lago di Braies, then hike directly to [Rifugio Fodara Vedla](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7fbK6Nyso8B5t9pAA). If bad in the afternoon: hike faster to Sennes/Fodara Vedla for lunch instead.
- The 9:00 or 9:30am bus reaches the trailhead ~10:00–10:30am. At a fast pace, Rifugio Biella is reached for lunch ~13:30–14:00; at a slower pace, ~14:00–15:00+.
- Alternative: eat lunch at Lago di Braies at ~11:00–11:30am, then hike 5+ hrs directly to Rifugio Fodara Vedla (more rushed).

After the traverse ends at Rifugio Croda da Lago on Sept 17, the group drives [Cortina → Bologna](https://maps.app.goo.gl/px9ywHuK8n5eg1gd9) on Sept 18 (~4 hrs) — see [[itinerary]].
