# Val di Funes

**Summary**: A scenic valley in South Tyrol, used as the base for the first two days of hiking (Sept 7–8).

**Sources**: 202609_dolomites.md

**Last updated**: 2026-09-06

---

Val di Funes (Villnöss in German) sits beneath the Odle/Geisler group. It is known for the church of Santa Maddalena (needs verification — not from source).

## Getting there (Sept 7)

On the way from Milan Linate, the group stops in Rovereto for lunch — [~2 hr drive from LIN](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/Milan+Linate+Airport,+Viale+Enrico+Forlanini,+20054+Segrate+MI,+Italy/Parcheggio+centro+storico,+Viale+dei+Colli,+3a,+38068+Rovereto+TN,+Italy) — at [Trattoria Bella Vista](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7LkAd4r7ZHcrtxwG6), then continues on to Val di Funes with a stop at a supermarket to stock up (source: 202609_dolomites.md). See [[transportation]] for the full LIN → Val di Funes drive.
