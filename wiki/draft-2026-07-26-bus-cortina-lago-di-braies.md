# Bus Times: Cortina → Lago di Braies (Sept 14)

**Summary**: Specific bus schedules and pre-booking requirements for reaching Lago di Braies on Sept 14, the start of the [[alta-via-1]] hike. Two buses are needed — Bus 445 to Dobbiaco, then Bus 442 to the lake — with a target arrival of 09:40–10:00.

**Sources**: raw/draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md

**Last updated**: 2026-07-26

---

## Route Overview

Getting from Cortina d'Ampezzo to Lago di Braies on Sept 14 requires two buses with a connection in Dobbiaco (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/).

Sept 14 falls within the **peak-season restricted window (July 1–September 15)**, so advance online booking is mandatory — you cannot simply board on the day (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.prags.bz/en).

---

## Leg 1: Bus 445 — Cortina d'Ampezzo → Dobbiaco

- **Departs**: Cortina (Scuole/Olympic Stadium) at **08:20** (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/)
- **Arrives**: Dobbiaco Autostazione at approximately **09:05**
- **Journey time**: ~45 minutes
- **Ticket**: ~€6.50 per person (2025 price; 2026 price needs confirmation)
- Cards and contactless accepted on board (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md)
- Only the first two daily departures are practical for [[alta-via-1]] trekkers
- Ask your Cortina hotel for a **South Tyrol Guest Card** — it grants free travel on Bus 445
- **Important**: stay on until the final stop, **Dobbiaco Autostazione** — do not exit early at Dobbiaco train station

---

## Leg 2: Bus 442 — Dobbiaco → Lago di Braies

- **After the 445 connection**: board Bus 442 at approximately **09:10–09:30**
- **Arrives Lago di Braies**: approximately **09:40–10:00**
- **Frequency**: roughly every 30 minutes throughout the day
- **Journey time**: ~30 minutes
- **Ticket**: ~€7 per person (2025 price; 2026 price not yet released) (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.prags.bz/en)

---

## Pre-Booking Requirement

Lago di Braies restricts bus access between July 1 and September 15. During this period, tickets must be booked and prepaid online (source: draft-2026-07-26-bus-cortina-lago-di-braies.md, url: https://www.prags.bz/en). Tickets typically go on sale around mid-June and sell out quickly.

- Pre-book Bus 442 at: https://www.prags.bz/en

---

## Recommended Departure Schedule

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 08:00 | Head to Cortina bus stop (Scuole/Stadio) |
| 08:20 | Board Bus 445 toward Dobbiaco |
| ~09:05 | Arrive Dobbiaco Autostazione |
| ~09:10–09:30 | Board Bus 442 toward Lago di Braies |
| ~09:40–10:00 | Arrive Lago di Braies — start hiking! |

Arriving by 10:00 gives a full hiking day and comfortable buffer before [Rifugio Fodara Vedla](https://maps.app.goo.gl/7fbK6Nyso8B5t9pAA) check-in at 14:00.

## Action Items

1. From mid-June: book Bus 442 pre-booked ticket at https://www.prags.bz/en — tickets sell out.
2. Closer to trip: confirm Bus 445 timetable at https://www.suedtirolmobil.info/en/#/.
3. On arrival in Cortina (Sept 13): ask hotel for a **South Tyrol Guest Card** for free Bus 445 travel.
